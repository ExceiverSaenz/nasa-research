## Question C01 and what was done

Is there evidence justifying combining tools? An 18-case matrix
was built with A03 members and availability, geometry interpreted from A02,
exported classes and A04 crop results. Each axis remains independent:
a well-separated box may lack a usable crop and a specific class
does not guarantee recovering all content. No weights or score are assigned.

A03 associations and measures were reused, without rerunning matching,
clustering, S01 or a full historical review. Judgments are the assistant's,
with referenced evidence and limitations, without independent human validation or GT.

## Interpretation

Useful diagnostic alternatives are observed, particularly figure/equation
separation and explicit categories in MinerU, and TATR table detection.
But no combination has been shown to outperform a single parser: shared errors,
different objectives and integration costs may exist.
Table crops and table captions still require review under the
chosen criteria. No rule that automatically chooses boxes is proposed.

LM retains list information and separate labels, useful table candidates
and several text regions. They are not discarded for score, coexisting errors or
lack of crop export. Nor is an artificial sum of records declared useful
for having more apparent content: that union has neither been created nor validated.

The A04 correction about r12 is explicitly propagated: it appears to continue
item2, not necessarily prose unrelated to the list. This is a contextual reading
hypothesis; labels are not replaced and reading order is not validated.

Full matrix: analytics/results/sic12_page3/a05-20261024/matrix.json;
manifest/status, recipe and verifier/export-check.json in the same folder.
18 cases preserve the three axes, 48 references and exact A04 statuses.

Technical traceability was verified, not the independent truth of each judgment.

No new production code or historical suites repeated for context.
No inference, fusion, expansion, GT, quality percentages or global winner.
Observed complementarity is potential and local; not a validated integration
policy or a result generalizable to the 14 SiC papers.
